# Figure 5 formal screening rule

Scored pool: N = 176 materials with C, K, R and W available.

Eligibility gate:
- W >= 0.725 (E3/E4 water-stability evidence threshold)
- K >= 0.60
- R >= 0.70

Eligible pool: N = 44.

Final design set: the ten highest PAI materials in the eligible pool. The dashed divider in Figure 5c is the Top-10 selection boundary, not a universal PAI threshold.
